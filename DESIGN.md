# Humans of NEO visual identity

## Style Prompt
A modern, youthful interview edit with natural full-frame portrait footage, original spoken audio, quiet editorial labels, and restrained mint accents. The speaker remains central.

## Colors
- #101C1A: caption backgrounds and title panels
- #F7F8F2: primary text
- #A6E5C7: accent text and department markers

## Typography
Be Vietnam Pro SemiBold, with Vietnamese diacritics. The font and its SIL Open Font License are included under assets/.

## Motion
Short fades and small vertical entrances. Subtle dissolves at editorial cuts. Preserve the natural interview aesthetic.

## What NOT to Do
- Invent dialogue, biographical facts, or supporting footage.
- Cover the speaker’s face with graphics.
- Crop tightly into low-resolution source footage.
- Add distracting effects or dramatic music.

## Editorial constraints
Final runtime below 90 seconds. Use verified local transcription for subtitles. Private captions and source timecodes belong in ignored edit.json, never in public source files.
